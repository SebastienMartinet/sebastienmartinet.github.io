About me

PhD in Astrophysics and specialist in Nuclear Astrophysics searching for new challenges

I am a postdoctoral researcher in astrophysics and nuclear physics with a strong background in computational modeling, data analysis, and statistical methods. Throughout my career, I have tackled complex problems by leveraging high-performance computing, machine learning techniques, and large-scale data processing.

My expertise in Python, R, and Fortran, combined with my experience in statistical analysis, Bayesian inference, and predictive modeling, makes me well-suited for data-driven roles. I have worked extensively with big data, numerical simulations, and algorithm development, skills that are directly applicable to data science and machine learning applications.

Beyond technical expertise, I excel in problem-solving, interdisciplinary collaboration, and scientific communication. Whether optimizing models for astrophysical simulations or extracting meaningful insights from large datasets, I am passionate about transforming data into actionable knowledge. Now, I am looking to transition into data science to apply my analytical skills in new and impactful ways.
